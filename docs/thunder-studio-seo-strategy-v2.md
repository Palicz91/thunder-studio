## Phase 6: Content Guidelines

### 6.1 Answer Block (CRITICAL for AI citation)

Every new content page MUST start with a 40-60 word answer block after H1, before the first H2.

**Template:**
```
[Direct answer to the page's primary question]. [One specific fact or number].
[Qualifier that prompts reading further].
```

**Example for "Music rehearsal rooms in Bangkok":**
> Bangkok has over 50 music rehearsal rooms, ranging from ฿150-800 per hour depending on room size and equipment. Most studios in Sukhumvit, Ladprao, and Bang Khen areas include Marshall amps, full drum kits, and PA systems. Here's how to find the right room for your band's needs and budget.

### 6.2 AI Citation Patterns (apply 2-3 per page)

| Pattern | Priority for Thunder Studio | Why |
|---------|---------------------------|-----|
| Definition block | ★★★★★ | "ห้องซ้อมดนตรี คืออะไร" has 16K AI vol with no dedicated page |
| FAQ block + schema | ★★★★★ | Already implemented — maintain on all new pages |
| Numbered steps | ★★★★ | How-to guides (recording, rehearsal prep) |
| Comparison table | ★★★★ | Room comparison, studio comparison |
| Statistic with attribution | ★★★ | "Bangkok has X music studios" — cite industry data |
| Expert authority signal | ★★ | Less applicable for a studio (not a thought leader) |

### 6.3 Content Standards (from v1 — maintained)

- Word count: Guides 2,000-4,000, Comparisons 1,500-2,500
- FAQ: 5-7 questions per page, matched to real search queries
- Internal links: 3-5 per page to related content
- CTA: Every page ends with "Book a Room"
- Sources: Cite facts with source name + year
- Update: Quarterly review

### 6.4 Bilingual Content Rules

- Thai is primary — write Thai first, then translate to English
- Do NOT use AI-style Thai (avoid สามารถ...ได้, em dashes, repeated ระดับมืออาชีพ)
- Natural conversational Thai, as validated in the Aug 6 Thai writing audit
- English version targets expat audience — more explicit context about Bangkok
