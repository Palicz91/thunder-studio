## What's Next — Priority Order

### #1 Priority: Google Business Profile (CRITICAL)

This is the single most important action. It captures 8,800 monthly searches that currently go nowhere for Thunder Studio.

**What it takes:** 1-2 hours of the owner's time to set up and verify at business.google.com

**What it gives:**
- Appear in Google Maps when anyone searches "ห้องซ้อมดนตรีใกล้ฉัน"
- Show photos, hours, phone number, reviews directly in search results
- Estimated impact: 1,300-2,200 new visitors per month from Maps alone

### #2: Google Search Console

30 minutes to set up. Lets us track which searches bring people to the website, and fix any indexing issues.

### #3: Pricing page

Musicians searching "ห้องซ้อมดนตรี ราคา" (rehearsal room price) and "ห้องซ้อมดนตรีใกล้ฉัน ราคาถูก" (affordable rehearsal room near me) need to see prices. Without a pricing page, they leave and go to a competitor who shows rates.

**Blocked:** We need the hourly rates per room from the studio.

### #4: Directory submissions

Submit Thunder Studio to:
1. **Google Business Profile** — owner action (see #1)
2. **Wongnai** — Thailand's biggest discovery platform
3. **Facebook Business Page** — essential for Thai audience
4. Thailand YellowPages, Longdo Map, OpenStreetMap

### #5: Review collection

Once GBP is live, every customer should be asked for a Google review. Studios with 20+ reviews rank significantly higher in Maps. A simple QR code at the front desk linking to the review page is all it takes.
